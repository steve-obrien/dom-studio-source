## Big Buck Bunny

Copyright (c) 2008 Blender Foundation | https://www.bigbuckbunny.org

- Film and licence statement: https://peach.blender.org/about/
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Download page: https://peach.blender.org/download/
- Source: https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/BBB/bbb_sunflower_1080p_30fps_normal.mp4.zip
- `bunny-meadow.mp4`: 01:00–01:16 of the source above (16 seconds).
- `bunny-forest.mp4`: 02:05–02:19 (14 seconds).

The source is the Sunflower 1080p/30fps release. DOM Studio cropped the centre
to portrait, resized to 540 × 960, and re-encoded to H.264/AAC. Original sound
is retained. The matching JPEG posters are frames 6 and 4 seconds into the
respective excerpts. The supplied excerpts contain no spoken dialogue.
